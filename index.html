01 / About

About

I work where circuit design meets systems engineering, and I like hardware best once it leaves the screen.

At UGA's Small Satellite Research Lab I've designed space-grade PCBs for a 2U CubeSat and now serve as Chief Engineer on the Learn-Sat mission, leading a 25+ person team through requirements, interface control, and design reviews.

Outside the lab I've worked on RF compliance for electric vehicles at Textron, client PCB design at CREATE Labs, and electrical architecture as a founding hardware engineer at Parallax Defense.
